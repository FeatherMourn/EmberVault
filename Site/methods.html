Methods

Methods explain how EmberVault turns a question into a bounded, reproducible record without confusing a source claim with a runtime result.

Core process

  1. Define the question and why it matters.
  2. Record build, runtime, inputs, assumptions, and safety limits.
  3. Run static inspection or a bounded read-only probe.
  4. Separate observations, interpretations, hypotheses, and unknowns.
  5. Record what worked, what failed, what was blocked, and the next experiment.
