Library category · Research process

Methods for making evidence you can trust.

Methods describe how EmberVault turns a question into a bounded, reproducible record. They keep static data, runtime observation, interpretation, and speculation separate.

Version boundary: The current research baseline is Enshrouded 1.0.0.3, source build 39026384728. Record the build and runtime for every new test.

The EmberVault workflow

01 · Define

Ask a bounded question

State what you want to know and why it matters.

02 · Prepare

Record the environment

Capture build, loader, inputs, assumptions, and safety limits.

03 · Test

Use the smallest probe

Start with static inspection or a read-only runtime observation.

04 · Separate

Label the evidence

Distinguish observation, interpretation, hypothesis, and unknown.

05 · Preserve

Record the path

Keep successes, failures, blocked attempts, outputs, and next steps.

Core principles

Evidence before assumptions.

  • Read-only by default.
  • Build-aware and environment-specific.
  • Reproducible from source and procedure.
  • Failures remain visible.
  • Unsupported capabilities are not promoted to facts.

Record checklist

Every experiment should answer:

  • What was the idea?
  • What did we try?
  • What worked?
  • What failed or was blocked?
  • What data supports the result?
  • What should be tried next?
