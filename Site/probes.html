Research instruments · source and purpose

Probe Library

Probes are bounded research instruments. Their source explains intended behavior; reports and exported output establish what actually happened in a specific build and environment.

Probe families

  • API and schema discovery — inventories services, resource types, fields, references, and scalar access.
  • Appearance and player state — investigates schemas, owner context, local-player access, and controlled no-op boundaries.
  • Inventory and item research — examines live inventory, equipment, item-instance schemas, and ownership relationships.
  • Localization and content structures — inventories tags and tests EML or ShroudForge resource access.
  • World, building, and event research — examines blueprints, voxel structures, and loot-pickup surfaces.

Documentation standard

Every probe record should explain its question, inputs, runtime assumptions, observations, output format, write boundary, failure modes, limitations, related reports, and next experiment. The source corpus and manifest summaries are staged under data/probes/.
