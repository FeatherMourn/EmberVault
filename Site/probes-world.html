World, Building, and Event Research

These probes inspect blueprint fields, building voxel structures, and loot-pickup surfaces while keeping live writes and persistence outside the default scope.

Probe breakdown

BLUEPRINTS

Blueprint Field Probe

Reads blueprint resources and field shapes; nested payloads may remain opaque.

Case file: blueprint runtime

VOXELS

Building Voxel Census

Checks building and voxel structures and classifies results as availability evidence.

EVENTS

Loot Pickup Census

Observes loot-pickup structures or events without claiming live ownership or persistence.

Technical boundary

Readable resources and fields do not establish safe voxel writes, save-container round trips, or live entity control.
