What can be observed, understood, and tested?

This page organizes EmberVault research by capability domain. A capability is not a promise that the game can be changed; it is a question about what the captured data, runtime, probes, or tools can demonstrate.

Version boundary: Unless a record says otherwise, this research applies to Enshrouded 1.0.0.3 and source build 39026384728. Results may change after game or loader updates.

How to read this map

Evidence before assumptions.

Static catalogs show that a resource, field, or relationship exists in captured data. Probes and runtime reports show what was observed in a specific environment. A capability becomes stronger only when multiple records agree and the limits are documented.

Evidence levels

  • Verified: explicitly supported by the source record.
  • Observed: seen in a particular build or run.
  • Static: present in catalog or schema data.
  • Blocked: the test could not establish the result.
  • Unclassified: requires further interpretation.
