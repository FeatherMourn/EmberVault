Library category · Case files

Experiments preserve the path.

An experiment is more than its final result. EmberVault records the idea, environment, procedure, observations, failures, interpretation, limitations, and next test so another researcher can understand what happened and continue the work.

Version boundary: These case files are associated with Enshrouded 1.0.0.3 and source build 39026384728 unless a record states otherwise.

The case-file lifecycle

01 · Question

What were we trying to learn?

State the problem, motivation, assumptions, and expected signal.

02 · Environment

What was tested?

Record game build, loader, tools, inputs, and safety boundaries.

03 · Procedure

What did we try?

Preserve the steps, probes, transformations, and reruns.

04 · Result

What happened?

Separate observed behavior from interpretation and hypothesis.

05 · Continuation

What comes next?

Document failures, blockers, limitations, and the next experiment.

What makes a useful experiment record?

Failures are data.

  • Do not erase blocked or negative results.
  • Do not promote a hypothesis to a capability.
  • Keep source paths and evidence attached.
  • Record the exact build and runtime.
  • State what the test did not establish.
