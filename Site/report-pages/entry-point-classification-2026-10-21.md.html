# Entry-Point DLL Classification

Date: 2026-10-21

## `dinput8.dll`

**Classification: active intentional forwarding chain.**

- Active root `dinput8.dll` is classified as the ModLoader Proxy.
- The proxy log records forwarding to `shroudforge-original/dinput8.dll`.
- The Shroud Forge copy is therefore an active forwarded target, not an
  independent competing bootstrap.
- Two additional copies are archived backups.
- One additional copy is a pending update.

This is the primary loader chain to preserve during future experiments.

## `dbghelp.dll`

**Classification: active EML candidate plus pending update.**

- Root `dbghelp.dll` is the active EML candidate.
- `shroudforge/updates/pending/dbghelp.dll` is not active and should not be
  treated as a second live EML loader.
- Same-process EML initialization with the active Shroud Forge chain remains
  unverified.

## `winmm.dll`

**Classification: active proxy candidate plus pending update.**

- Root `winmm.dll` is an active candidate and is not listed in the current
  `chainloader.ini` load list.
- `shroudforge/updates/pending/winmm.dll` is a pending copy.
- No current evidence proves that root `winmm.dll` is participating in the
  active Shroud Forge/ModLoader Proxy chain.

It should be investigated separately rather than assumed to be a conflict.

## Conclusion

The inventory currently supports one confirmed intentional `dinput8.dll`
forwarding chain. `dbghelp.dll` and `winmm.dll` are active candidates, but
their relationship to the running chain needs runtime evidence. Archived and
pending copies are excluded from active-collision decisions.
