# Master roadmap completion audit

Date: 2026-10-23

## Audit result

The current audit promotes no additional stages. Stages 0–3 remain the only
stages at 100% completion for their explicitly defined scopes. The later
stages have useful implementations and evidence, but still have stated exit
criteria that are not met.

## 100% complete stages

### Stage 0 — Research record and provenance foundation

Marked **100% complete for the defined provenance foundation**. Ongoing
provenance maintenance is a standing obligation, not an unfinished closure
criterion.

### Stage 1 — Static schema and asset census

Marked **100% complete for the defined static census scope**. Runtime
ownership, persistence, localization decoding, and editor behavior are
explicitly downstream rather than missing Stage 1 requirements.

### Stage 2 — ShroudForge baseline and private fork

Marked **100% complete for the private baseline and recovery scope**. Matching
artifacts, deny policy, package checks, patch-set provenance, restoration, and
startup smoke evidence are recorded.

### Stage 3 — Provider runtime foundation

Marked **100% complete for its defined offline foundation scope**. The
provider contract, shared snapshot, readiness separation, status model,
dependency gates, lifecycle states, handle safety, fault isolation, telemetry,
budgets, scheduling, provenance, and cross-layer fixtures are implemented and
validated. Live player ownership is intentionally downstream work in Stages 5
and 6, so its unresolved state does not reopen Stage 3.

## Stages not marked 100%

- Stage 4 still lacks the final private-world smoke and worker-contract tests.
- Stage 5 still lacks a repeatable unique player-to-inventory relationship.
- Stage 6 depends on Stage 5 and has partial domain evidence.
- Stages 7–9 have implemented foundations but require coverage or integration.
- Stage 10 is intentionally gated until read/ownership contracts are proven.
- Stage 11 remains planned.
