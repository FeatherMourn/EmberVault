read-only save-copy research. It does not claim that represented fields are

## Save-copy findings

- Character and world snapshots are KSC1 binary containers.
- Copies were made before inspection; originals remain untouched.
- Existing research identifies character and inventory-related regions, but no
  item-instance round trip has been decoded or validated.
- No checksum, compression, serializer, or persistence contract should be
  assumed from the current byte-level observations.

## Capability classification

- **Represented:** confirmed by reflection or static catalog evidence.
- **Runtime-readable:** not established for player-owned item instances.
- **Writable:** not established; no item-instance write was attempted.
- **Persistent:** not established; save round-trip remains unproven.
- **Next safe static target:** decode the KSC1 copy structure and correlate
  candidate item regions with the reflected field map without editing bytes.

## Offline KSC1 correlation result

The read-only character-copy inspection found an active `characters-8` KSC1
container with 34 blobs. Its table contains repeated `CHAR`, `FOWR`, and
`COUT` records plus a `KNPL` record and four-byte binary tags that are not yet
identified. The existing decoder can validate the container and enumerate its
blob table, but it does not yet decompress or interpret the character payloads
that could contain item instances.

A bounded byte-string search found no plain-text field names such as item,
equipment, perk, gem, or slot identifiers. That is consistent with compressed
or serialized payloads; it is not evidence that those fields are absent.

**Updated classification:** KSC1 structure is readable; blob categories are
partially classified; item-instance field correlation is still unknown; no
bytes were changed and no round-trip or persistence claim is made.
