Inventory runtime access

Can a read-only runtime probe observe player inventory and item data?

Status: PartialEvidence: ObservedBuild: 1.0Runtime: ShroudForge

What we wanted to learn

Establish whether inventory-aware research tools are feasible without assuming that visible data can be safely modified.

What we tried

  1. Inspected static inventory schemas.
  2. Ran the live inventory probe.
  3. Compared entity and item observations.
  4. Tested ownership and equipment relationships.

What worked

Inventory entities and item data were observed.

The read-only probe provided useful runtime observations for capability mapping.

What failed or remained unresolved

Ownership and equipment relationships remained incomplete.

The available evidence was not sufficient to claim complete ownership mapping or safe item editing.

What we learned

Runtime inventory visibility is partially established. The observation boundary is useful, but the relationship between inventory, ownership, and equipment needs additional validation.

Next experiment

Test additional entity contexts and compare the results across builds while preserving read-only safety and rollback evidence.
