Probe family · Inventory and item research

Inventory and Item Research

These probes examine item schemas, inventory structures, equipment relationships, and the missing bridge between reflected ownership data and live player instances.

Probe breakdown

LIVE ACCESS

Live Inventory Probe

Attempts bounded observation of live inventory entities and item data after runtime initialization.

Case file: inventory runtime access

ECS

Inventory ECS Probe

Inspects inventory-related ECS structures and records whether expected components or links are available.

SCHEMA

Item Instance Schema Probe

Maps item-instance fields and nested values without assuming that a static instance is a live owned item.

OWNERSHIP

Equipment Ownership Probe

Investigates equipment links and ownership relationships; unresolved entity conversion remains a central limitation.

Technical boundary

These probes are read-oriented and bounded. They do not enumerate arbitrary entities, save changes, or establish a player-to-item identity chain without supporting runtime evidence.
