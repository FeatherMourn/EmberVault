Why this fork exists

A research instrument, not just a collection of mods.

The downstream ShroudForge fork gives EmberVault a controlled way to investigate the Enshrouded runtime. The purpose is not to claim unsupported capabilities or provide automatic permission to change live game state. The purpose is to build a trustworthy map of the modding environment.
