# Recipe workshop coverage

The 2,536 static recipes were grouped by their workshop ID and joined to the verified workshop-to-ItemInfo fallback map.

- 46 workshop groups are used by recipes.
- 41 groups have a station or NPC debug-name fallback.
- 5 groups remain unnamed statically; this includes the default workshop group and records without an item reference.

The largest recipe groups are associated with the carpenter crafter, carpenter workbench, hunter crafter, alchemist crafter, blacksmith crafter, and the general workbench. Each group retains its numeric workshop ID and localization ID where available.

This is static same-build evidence and does not depend on the unavailable EML resource-read profile.
